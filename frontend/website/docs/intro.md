# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive course on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This course explores the intersection of artificial intelligence and robotics, focusing on embodied intelligence that bridges digital AI with physical humanoid robots.

## Course Overview

This capstone quarter course covers the future of AI in the physical world using ROS 2, Gazebo, and NVIDIA Isaac for design, simulation, and deployment.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Understand the fundamentals of ROS 2 and its role in robotic systems
- Design and simulate robotic systems using Gazebo and Unity
- Implement AI-driven navigation and control systems using NVIDIA Isaac
- Create vision-language-action systems that can respond to voice commands
- Build a complete autonomous humanoid robot system

## Course Structure

The course is organized into four main modules:

1. **The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)** - Learn about ROS 2 Nodes/Topics/Services, bridging Python agents with rclpy, and URDF for humanoids
2. **The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)** - Explore physics simulation, collisions, high-fidelity rendering, and sensors
3. **The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)** - Work with Isaac Sim for photorealistic sim and synthetic data, Isaac ROS for VSLAM/navigation, and Nav2 for bipedal movement
4. **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** - Implement voice-to-action with OpenAI Whisper and cognitive planning with LLMs

Let's begin our journey into Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics!
